On the Intrinsic Dimensions of Data in Kernel Learning

Rustem Takhanov

Paper PDFarXivCodePosterSlides

About this paper

How does the geometry of data influence kernel learning? Two notions of intrinsic dimension offer complementary answers.

The analysis connects kernel-induced Minkowski dimension, Kolmogorov widths, and eigenvalue decay. It derives learning guarantees, proposes a sample-based estimation method, and shows that effective and geometric dimensions can differ on fractal domains.
