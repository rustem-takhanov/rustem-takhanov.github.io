Syllable-aware Neural Language Models: A Failure to Beat Character-aware Ones

Zhenisbek Assylbekov, Rustem Takhanov, Bagdat Myrzakhmetov, Jonathan N. Washington

Paper PDFACL AnthologyGoogle Scholar

About this paper

Compares syllable-based and character-based word representations for recurrent neural language models.

Syllable segmentation does not improve modeling quality over character segmentation in the reported experiments. The best syllable-aware model nevertheless achieves comparable performance with 18–33% fewer parameters and trains 1.2–2.2 times faster.
