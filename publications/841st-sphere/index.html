Rustem Takhanov, Zhenisbek Assylbekov, Stanislav Yun

Kissing arrangements in ℝ¹² · exact start of the search

Two flexible blocks, 720 fixed bridges, one more sphere

The 840-point arrangements studied here split ℝ¹² into two copies of ℝ⁶. Each copy holds a 60-point block, 12 axial points and a 48-system, and 720 bridge points put half of their length in each copy. The bridges stay put while each 48-system can turn. That freedom gives infinitely many 840-point arrangements and the starting point from which an 841st sphere was found.

About this paper

Uses flexibility in 840-point arrangements to guide optimization toward an 841-point kissing arrangement in twelve dimensions.

The construction keeps 720 bridge vectors fixed while varying two 60-point blocks. This geometric freedom provides structured initializations for logarithmic Riesz energy optimization and leads to a numerical 841-point configuration.
