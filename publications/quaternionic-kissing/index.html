A quaternionic construction behind 841-point kissing arrangement in ℝ¹²

Rustem Takhanov

ℝ¹² as ℍ³ · the 840-point quaternionic model, exact

Where the 840 points put their weight

Write a point of ℝ¹² as three quaternions. The 840-point model is three axial 24-cells, one in each factor, and two families of 384 whose squared length splits as (½, ¼, ¼) and (¼, ½, ¼). The third factor carries the least weight, and that is where the numerical 841-point arrangement finds its room.

About this paper

A quaternionic viewpoint reveals the structure behind a numerical 841-point kissing arrangement in twelve dimensions.

Viewing twelve-dimensional space as three quaternionic factors leads to a new 840-point model built from three orthogonal 24-cells and two 384-point families. Asymmetry between the factors helps explain the geometry of the numerical 841-point arrangement.
