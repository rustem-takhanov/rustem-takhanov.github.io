Latin cubes of order 4 and 4320-point quaternionic constructions in ℝ¹⁶

Rustem Takhanov

To appear in the AIST 2026 proceedings, in Springer’s Communications in Computer and Information Science (CCIS) series.

Paper PDFarXivAIST 2026

About this paper

Constructs a family of 4,320-point kissing arrangements in sixteen dimensions using Latin cubes of order four and permutations of quaternionic levels.

All arrangements in the family are antipodal and infinitesimally jammed, and their frame operator is 270 times the identity. A computer-aided enumeration gives exactly 4,072,728,621 isometry classes, revealing a vast diversity of configurations with the same size as the Barnes–Wall arrangement.
