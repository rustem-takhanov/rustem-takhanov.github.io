Autoencoders for a manifold learning problem with a Jacobian rank constraint

Rustem Takhanov, Y. Sultan Abylkairov, Maxat Tezekbayev

Paper PDFarXivPattern RecognitionCode

About this paper

Uses Jacobian rank constraints to encourage autoencoders to learn low-dimensional manifolds.

Manifold learning is formulated as finding a correcting map onto a hidden manifold. A Jacobian penalty and a curvature term supplement the architectural bottleneck, with experiments on synthetic and real datasets.
