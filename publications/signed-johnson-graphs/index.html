Classification of independent sets in signed Johnson graphs and applications to kissing arrangements

Rustem Takhanov, Stanislav Yun

Paper PDFarXiv

Signed Johnson graphs · exact drawings

From independent sets to 840 kissing points

A vertex of J±(n, 4) is a vector with four entries ±1 and the rest 0; two vertices are joined exactly when their caps would overlap. A maximum independent set, together with the 2n axial points ±2eᵢ, is therefore a kissing arrangement. In ℝ¹² most of the 1579 maximum independent sets come from one recipe built on the complete graph K₆.

Axial points 24

±2eᵢ for the twelve coordinates. A block’s 16 points and its 8 axial points form a 24-cell.

Bridge blocks 45 × 16

An edge {x, y} on the left and an edge {z′, t′} of the same colour on the right.

Free zones 2 × 48

Any maximum independent set of J±(6, 4) in each copy. These choices give 1157 of the 1579 classes.

Reading the drawings

  • Exact. Every count, colour class and slice is computed from the definitions. The 840-point arrangements behind the drawings pass a check of all 352,380 inner products; the largest is ½.
  • Globes. A globe is a genuine 3-dimensional slice of ℝ¹². Its caps have their true angular radius, 30°, and touch at 60°; dashed circles are empty sites.
  • Hubs. Each coloured hub stands for the 3 × 3 pairings of its colour; it is not a point of the arrangement.
  • 4-cube. The 16 points of a block differ in signs only. Points one sign apart touch; two signs apart they are orthogonal.

About this paper

Classifies maximum independent sets in signed Johnson graphs and reveals a rich family of kissing arrangements.

An enumeration up to signed permutations finds 1,579 non-isometric 840-point arrangements in twelve dimensions. Structural results connect these independent sets with constant-weight codes and Steiner quadruple systems.
