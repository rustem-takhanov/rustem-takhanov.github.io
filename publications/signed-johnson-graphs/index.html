Classification of independent sets in signed Johnson graphs and applications to kissing arrangements

Rustem Takhanov, Stanislav Yun

Paper PDFarXiv

About this paper

Classifies maximum independent sets in signed Johnson graphs and reveals a rich family of kissing arrangements.

An enumeration up to signed permutations finds 1,579 non-isometric 840-point arrangements in twelve dimensions. Structural results connect these independent sets with constant-weight codes and Steiner quadruple systems.
