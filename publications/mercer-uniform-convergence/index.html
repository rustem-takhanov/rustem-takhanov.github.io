On the speed of uniform convergence in Mercer’s theorem

Rustem Takhanov

Paper PDFarXivJournal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications

About this paper

Quantifies how quickly truncated Mercer expansions converge uniformly to a positive definite kernel.

The convergence estimates depend on eigenvalue tails and kernel smoothness. The results also give spectral information about integral operators whose roots and other powers have continuous kernels.
