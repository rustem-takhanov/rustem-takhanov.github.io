Optimization Methods and Techniques
  • Nonlinear programming: maxima and minima; first- and second-order conditions.
  • Numerical methods for unconstrained optimization: Newton and quasi-Newton methods.
  • Constrained optimization: optimality conditions for equality and inequality constraints.
  • Gradient projection, Lagrangian relaxation, and penalty methods.
  • Linear programming: polyhedra, extreme points, vertices, and basic feasible solutions.
  • The simplex method, sensitivity analysis, and strong and weak duality.
  • Network flows, the network simplex algorithm, maximum flows, assignment, and shortest paths.
  • Integer programming: exact and approximate methods, relaxation, and branch and bound.
  • Multi-objective optimization: partial orders, efficiency, scalarization, and applications.
  • Goal programming: principal results and applications.
