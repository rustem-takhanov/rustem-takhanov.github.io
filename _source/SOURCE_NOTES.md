The 24-cell illustration is an exact 2D orthogonal projection of the 8 signed basis vectors and 16 vectors (±1/2, ±1/2, ±1/2, ±1/2). Edges connect inner product 1/2; there are 24 vertices and 96 edges. It is labeled as one four-dimensional building block, not as the 841-point configuration.

On 2026-10-28, an original SVG illustration was added before the summary on each of the 29 publication detail pages. The illustrations are generated by the dependency-free Python modules in `illustrations/`; none are copied paper figures. Each has alternative text, a caption, a link to its primary research source, and a full-size view. Captions distinguish conceptual diagrams from exact mathematical examples. Schematic plots and point clouds are explanatory rather than reported experiments. Examples such as the signed Johnson graph, 24-cell projections, Boolean CSPs, reflection, Mercer expansion, and pairwise-independent variables are constructed directly from their defining mathematics. The dimensions 25–31 diagram labels its numerical values as bounds in the linked paper, rather than asserting that they remain current records.

Later on 2026-10-28, the four kissing-number pages (signed Johnson graphs, the 841st sphere, the quaternionic construction, and dimensions 25–31) received figure plates in the style of the Cohn–Li Cap Atlas, replacing their single illustrations. Each plate has a main figure, a ledger of parts, one or two companion figures, and notes on reading them. Everything drawn is rebuilt from the papers' definitions and checked before drawing: Construction 1 for n = 12 (classical and glued free zones), the starting configuration of the 841 search with Example 1 in both blocks, the quaternionic 840-point model (inner-product table reproduced), and an exact three-dimensional slice of the Leech lifting lemma. The slack angles for dimensions 29 and 31 were recomputed from the certificate package at github.com/k-nic/kissing_d25-31. For the numerical 841-point arrangement the plates quote only values stated in the papers (largest inner product 0.499999937751; alignment residuals 0.048 and 0.55); its coordinates are not drawn.
