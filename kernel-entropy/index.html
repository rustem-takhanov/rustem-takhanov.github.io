Rustem Takhanov

Paper PDFarXiv

About this paper

Spectral bounds on the ε-entropy of kernel classes, linking approximation complexity to kernel eigenvalues.

The paper studies upper and lower bounds for unit balls of reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces. Eigenvalue decay and convergence of truncated Mercer expansions govern the bounds in different function norms.
